Tooth 11

Based on energetic interrelationships identified by Dr. Voll and Dr. Kramer (electroacupuncture):

  • Organs: Kidney, bladder, urogenital region; Sensory organs/paranasal sinuses: Ear, frontal sinus
  • Lymphatic pharyngeal ring: Tonsilla pharyngea (pharyngeal tonsil)
  • Joints: Back of the knee, foot
  • Vertebrae: 3rd lumbar vertebra, 2nd L, lower sacrum (S3, S4, S5), coccyx (Co)
  • Spinal segments: L3, L2, S5, S4, Co

Expression and function of the “Kidney/Bladder” functional circle in Traditional Chinese Medicine:

  • Stores innate resources, the so-called “hereditary energy,” and the constitution; governs growth and reproduction. All vitality has its foundation here.
  • The sensory function of the ear—including listening, hearing, protection against threats, warning, and alertness (Yang aspect).
  • The function of the skeletal system (including the jawbone and tooth sockets!)—also in the sense of stability (both physical and emotional), endurance, and security (Yin aspect).
  • The “Kidney/Bladder” functional circle is susceptible to fear, fright, and shock as sudden disruptions in life (“Fright goes right to the bones,” “Shock hits the kidneys,” “wet one’s pants out of fear”).
  • Keywords: stability, backbone, security, willpower, genetic predispositions, reproduction, lower abdomen, fear, winter.

Manifestation and function of the “Lung/Large Intestine” functional circle in Traditional Chinese Medicine:

  • Associated symptoms include flu-like “infections,” spontaneous sweating, shortness of breath, hoarseness, coughing, diarrhea and constipation, and skin diseases of all kinds.
  • Keywords: rhythm, breathing, nose, skin, communication, separation, grief, fall.

Expression and content of the “Spleen/Stomach” functional circle in Traditional Chinese Medicine:

  • The sensory function of the “tasting tongue,” the lips, and the mouth—in the sense of taking in, grasping/evaluating, and breaking down food (Yang aspect). Also, curiosity, communication with the environment and fellow human beings, and the ability to analyze, think, recognize, and judge.
  • The function of connective tissue in supplying and removing waste from the body’s cells, as well as in transforming and integrating the new or foreign into the self (Yin aspect). This applies not only to digestion and distribution but also to mental information processing.
  • Another important aspect is the “support function”: the spleen and stomach hold the organs in place, keep the body in shape, and keep the blood within the vessels. For example, bloating, spider veins, and a tendency to bruise indicate a weakness in this functional circle.
  • An unhealthy diet, environmental toxins, emotional overload (“that weighs heavily on my stomach”), endless brooding without insight, and negative expectations place a strain on the spleen/stomach functional circle.
  • Associated symptoms include fatigue, indigestion, pain in the stomach area, vomiting, belching, diarrhea, connective tissue weakness, certain types of gynecological discharge, and menstrual disorders.
  • Keywords: communication, integration, digestion, categorization, gathering, thinking, brooding.
